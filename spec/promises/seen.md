# Frames that were looked at

_Part of the promise book (`../promises.md`). A frame counts only when someone has looked at it with
the promise's words beside it and written down the weakest thing in it. The copy that was looked at
is in `seen/`. If a later build draws something else, the rung falls until the new frame is looked
at._

| frame | looked at | the weakest thing seen |
|---|---|---|
| `dark-ring` | 2026-11-19, Fable | The ring and what it has passed read at once, and nothing ahead of it is lit. The afterglow behind it is faint bands, Hum is a small ring with no body, and every thing is the same lollipop. |
| `asters-far` | 2026-11-19, Fable | Hum is small and the horizon is low, so the near aster reads as huge. The far ones are faint beside it, and nothing but the horizon and Hum says they are a hundred metres tall and not ten. The sun reads as dashed rings. |

| `asters-answer` | 2026-11-19, Fable | The crown lights in its own colours and the halos help it read. It is small on the screen; a neighbour's crown at the left edge reads better than the great one's. |
