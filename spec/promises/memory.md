# Promises of the card's memory

_Part of the promise book (`../promises.md`). What `../size.md` says the card is for._

### `card.ground` The ground remembers

- **Said:** `spec/size.md` "This is the one picture on the card, and the player paints it."
  "Wherever something sounds, it leaves its light on the ground beneath it, in its note's colour."
  "With the lake low the light fades in a breath, as trails do now." "Each river that is freed lets
  the ground hold more, and with the lake full it stays for good." "Over months the black ground
  becomes a slow photograph of everything that has been sung on it" "Under it is the old light,
  which was there before you" "A friend who visits leaves their colour on your ground for as long as
  the card lasts."
- **Stage:** 2
- **Scene:** asters
- **The moment:** You come back to the field the next day. Before you call, before anything rings,
  the ground is not black. There is a soft path across it where you walked yesterday. There is a
  pool of green where you stood and called. Under the great aster there is a wide stain in its
  bells' own colours, where they rang over you. You walk your own path back to it without thinking,
  the way you would follow a track in grass. Months on, the whole garden is a slow photograph of
  your time in it, and you can tell which corners you have never stood in because they are still
  black.
- **Far:** from a crown, or with the place laid down as a page, the ground is a map nobody drew:
  everywhere you have been, in the colour of what sounded there.
- **At its foot:** your own path under you, brighter where you stopped to sing.
- **After:** you choose where to walk partly by where you have not left any light yet.
- **A cheat would be:** footprints that fade in a few seconds; a glowing mark under a few special
  things; a map screen.
- **Full size:**
  - `frame asters-ground` Hum's path as a band of light, and the light left where it called at the
    aster's foot.
  - `fact handheld.asters.late == 0` With the ground's picture drawn under the lines, every frame of
    the walk is on time.
  - `owed` It is still there the next day: the picture is kept in a file when a person leaves, and a
    test saves one and loads it back the same. (It is kept now; the test is not written.)
  - `owed` One picture for each piece of land, 25 cm to a dot, loaded as Hum walks. The proof is one
    picture for the whole field, 62 cm to a dot.
  - `owed` The lake's level decides how long light stays: a breath when it is low, for good when it
    is full.
  - `owed` The old light: the rounds of the lives, worn into the ground where each one walks.
  - `owed` A friend's colour, left by a visit.
  - `owed` Laid down as a page, the ground's light is the map.
- **Asks:** Coming back to it, could you see where you had been?
