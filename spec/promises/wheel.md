_Part of the promise book (`../promises.md`). What `../../SPEC.md` section 9 says, sized before it
is
built. The wheel toy (`toys/wheel/`) tried most of it; Morgan, 2026-11-20, after playing it: "I

### `wheel.home` The wheel decides where home is

- **Said:** `SPEC.md` "You can sing one note. The wheel decides where home is. Seven lit wedges are
  seven moods and seven powers: wonder (Lift), sunny home (Bind), drive (Drive), cool soul (Flow),
  tenderness (Remember), fierceness (Strike), the darkest (Break)." "They never move under the
  thumb." "The ring of twelve notes turns behind them one notch each morning, so the wheel is also a
  compass, a calendar and a sky."
- **Stage:** 2
- **Scene:** asters
- **The moment:** (From the room: once the day has come up, hold R1, point the right stick a
  twelfth of a turn left, and let go.) You hold R1 and the world slows. Twelve wedges open on the
  ground around you,
  seven
  of them lit, and a thread runs from every creature in sight to the wedge that would bring it home.
  You point at the wedge the stumbling knot's threads all run to and let go. Home moves, and every
  shape on the screen changes at once: the knot's two strands, a pretzel a moment ago, are a hoop.
- **A cheat would be:** a menu of seven choices; a colour filter over the same world; wedges that
  slide round under the thumb.
- **Full size:**
  - `test home moves on the wheel, Hum's note with it, and what answers a call changes` Hum sings
    home's note, and moving home changes what answers.
  - `test R1 held opens the wheel; the right stick points at a wedge; letting go puts home there`
    Only an awake mood can be home; on the first days, wonder and sunny home.
  - `owed` A test: The seven lit wedges stay put while the twelve notes turn a notch each morning.
    The moods
    are where the thumb learned them; the notes behind them are the morning's.
  - `frame evening-wheel` The wheel open on the ground round Hum, the threads running to it.
  - `owed` The wheel read as a compass, a calendar and a sky: the morning's notch points at the
    land the day belongs to, and the sky's ring turns with it (`sky.md`).
- **Asks:** When the wheel opened, could you see where home would go before you let go?

### `wheel.groove` Each mood is a groove

- **Said:** `SPEC.md` "Each mood is also a groove. Seven lit of twelve is the pattern of the scale
  and
  of a bell rhythm played across West and Central Africa." "Where home sits decides where the
  pattern starts, so each mood has its own feel and each land's creatures move in it."
- **Stage:** 2
- **The moment:** Home on sunny home, and the field's footfalls fall in an easy lilt. Move home two
  wedges, and the same twelve-pulse pattern starts from another place: the creatures lurch forward
  in drive. Nobody told you the mood had a rhythm. Your foot found it.
- **A cheat would be:** seven drum loops, one for each mood.
- **Full size:**
  - `owed` A test: A mood's groove is the lit wedges as a twelve-pulse bell pattern starting from
    home. The
    seven lit of twelve, 2-2-1-2-2-2-1, read round from where home sits.
  - `owed` Each land's creatures step, strike and breathe in the groove of the mood home is in;
    measured from their onsets in a take (`ears`).
- **Asks:** Did each mood feel different in your body, not only in your ears?

### `wheel.borrow` The dark wedges

- **Said:** `SPEC.md` "Five dark wedges are borrowing. Reach under the horizon and home visits a
  note
  that has set, for one phrase, and the land comes with it." "The first borrow, home on the third
  morning, is free."
- **Stage:** 3
- **The moment:** You push the wheel past the last lit wedge, under the horizon. For one phrase home
  is a note that has set: the sky darkens toward it, the ground's colours shift, a creature that
  belongs to that note steps out of nowhere. The phrase ends and the land falls back, a little
  changed.
- **A cheat would be:** a sixth mood that is just another colour.
- **Full size:**
  - `owed` A test: A borrow lasts one phrase and home comes back. Home goes under, for the length of
    the
    phrase it was reached in, and returns.
  - `owed` A test: The first borrow on the third morning costs no breath.
  - `owed` The land comes with it: sky, ground and who is about, for that phrase.
- **Asks:** When you borrowed, did it feel like visiting somewhere, or like a trick?

### `voices.five` Five voices

- **Said:** `SPEC.md` "Five voices are who sings, bass to treble: Doorstop (oo), Drip (oh), Box
  Elder
  (ah), Puffball (eh), Patio (ee)." "Low voices move heavy things and the ground; high ones move
  light things, the air and you." "Each voice's overtones decide what it can reach (law 1)." "Small
  voices. Each life you help lends a colour of one of the five."
- **Stage:** 2
- **The moment:** At the bridge puddle you hum in Doorstop's oo, and the water stands in a ring. You
  change to Patio's ee and the same note draws a star of spokes: the bright vowel has the overtones
  that reach the higher shape. The ground hums under Doorstop; a leaf lifts under Patio.
- **A cheat would be:** five sound sets with the same effects; a stat that says which voice moves
  what.
- **Full size:**
  - `owed` A test: Each voice's overtones are its vowel's. Each voice's overtones are measured from
    its own
    vowel filter, as Hum's "mm" is (`game/src/world/pools.zig`, `hum_overtones`).
  - `owed` A test: A puddle sung in another voice stands in another shape. Law 1, through the
    overtones: no
    rule says which voice moves which thing.
  - `owed` Low voices move heavy things and the ground; high ones light things, the air and Hum:
    measured as how far each voice moves a stone, a leaf and Hum.
  - `owed` Small voices: each life helped lends a colour of one of the five (`people.md`).
- **Asks:** Did changing voice feel like changing what you could do?

### `swell.walk` The swell walks home

- **Said:** `SPEC.md` "The swell (hold R2) grows a colour each bar." "A nudge on the downbeat walks
  home one wedge and brings what you pass with it." "Where home is when you let go decides which of
  four cadences lands: the full close, the amen, the turn, the slide."
- **Stage:** 2
- **The moment:** Three creatures stand on a run of fifths. You hold R2 and Hum swells, a band of
  colour each bar. On each downbeat you nudge, and home steps one wedge, bringing the next creature
  home and greying the one after. You let go on the one after the last: the full close lands, and
  all three are at rest.
- **A cheat would be:** a combo meter; nudges counted at any time, off the beat.
- **Full size:**
  - `test the swell: a nudge on a downbeat walks home one wedge, and the let-go on the one lands the
    cadence`
    A nudge off the beat does nothing; on the downbeat it walks home one wedge; let go on the one
    with home a fifth down and the full close lands, a fifth up and the amen.
  - `owed` The turn and the slide, tested as the full close and the amen are.
  - `owed` What home passes comes with it: each creature on the way is brought home in turn.
- **Asks:** Did walking home on the downbeats feel like playing, or like pressing buttons in time?

### `swell.touch` Push hard for bright

- **Said:** `SPEC.md` "Push hard for bright, gently for tender. How far the stick is pushed is how
  loud and bright the note is."
- **Stage:** 2
- **The moment:** Singing to the sleeping knot, you ease the stick only a little and Hum's note
  comes
  out soft and round. Against the wolves you push it all the way and the same note is loud and
  bright, its upper overtones lit.
- **A cheat would be:** two volume levels.
- **Full size:**
  - `owed` A test: How far the stick is pushed sets how loud and how bright the held note is.
    Loudness and
    brightness rise together, smoothly, from the stick's first movement to its edge.
  - `owed` Measured with `ears`: the overtones above the third are 12 dB stronger at full push than
    at a quarter.
- **Asks:** Could you sing tenderly when you meant to?

### `swell.lean` The lean

- **Said:** `SPEC.md` "The lean. From the fifth morning, a half push toward a neighbouring wedge
  bends
  the held note toward it." "It always resolves when you let go."
- **Stage:** 3
- **The moment:** At the Stage at 2am you hold a note and half-push toward the next wedge. The note
  leans, a blue note, rough for a moment, and the band leans with you. You let go and it falls home.
- **A cheat would be:** a pitch bend that stays where it is left.
- **Full size:**
  - `owed` A test: A half push bends the held note toward the neighbour and letting go brings it
    home.
  - `owed` Measured with `ears`: the roughness of a lean is brief and resolves (`laws.md`, "For the
    ears").
- **Asks:** When you leaned, did it ache the right amount?

### `hands.told-apart` R1 and R2 told apart

- **Said:** `SPEC.md` "R1 and R2 are told apart at a glance and in the hands." "R1 is the world's;
  R2
  is Hum's own." "R1 held, the mood wheel, jumps home to any wedge while time thickens. The place
  lies back toward the page, and the wheel is drawn large on the ground round Hum in the ground's
  cool light, with a thread from every thing to the wedge that would bring it home." "What you hear
  stretches into a held chord." "R2 held, the swell, steps home only to a neighbour, on a downbeat,
  in time. Nothing is drawn on the ground." "Hum itself swells and takes a band of colour each bar,
  and the two neighbouring wedges show as two small marks either side of Hum, lit on each downbeat."
  "The view stays at Hum's height."
- **Stage:** 2
- **The moment:** Hold R1 and the world steps back: time thickens, the view lifts, the wheel spreads
  across the ground in cool light and every sound holds as one chord while you choose. Hold R2 and
  the world carries on: nothing is drawn on the ground, Hum swells warm in a band of colour each
  bar, and two small marks either side of it light on each downbeat, where a nudge would go. One is
  stopping to think; the other is playing. Your hands never mix them up.
- **A cheat would be:** the same wheel drawn for both, in another colour.
- **Full size:**
  - `test R1 held thickens time: the beat runs at a quarter, and lets go when the wheel closes` The
    world's beat, Drip's ticks, the bee, rings and the water run at a quarter while the wheel is
    open, and what is ringing stretches into a held chord; the swell keeps the full pace.
  - `test R1 held opens the wheel; the right stick points at a wedge; letting go puts home there`
    R1 lets home jump to any awake wedge; the swell's test shows R2 stepping only to a neighbour.
  - `frame evening-wheel` R1 held: the wheel large on the ground from above, threads to it.
  - `frame evening-swell` R2 held: Hum swelling with its marks, nothing on the ground.
  - `owed` Measured with `ears`: the amen's chord (clip 36) lands consonant, and Morgan's answer to
    it in the room.
  - `owed` Morgan's call on the proposal in `DECISIONS.md`: the swell's steps on L1 and L2, so the
    two share no stick.
- **Asks:** Could you tell R1 from R2 without thinking, by what you saw and what your hands did?

### `breath.one` One breath

- **Said:** `SPEC.md` "One breath pays for singing, the wheels, sprint and glide, and silence
  refills
  it." "Dodging and guarding are free." "A breath holds three homes at first and grows by one each
  time you land a longer walk, up to seven; the ferry makes eight."
- **Stage:** 2
- **The moment:** Halfway through a long walk home the swell thins: your breath is running out. You
  let go early, stand silent for a moment while it fills, and go again. Weeks later you land a walk
  of seven homes in one breath, and the breath grows.
- **A cheat would be:** a stamina bar that refills on its own while you keep singing.
- **Full size:**
  - `test one breath: holding a note is free at first, then slow; silence refills it; dodging costs
    nothing` A home of breath is what one step home in the swell costs. A held note is free for
    two bars, then an eighth of a home a bar; nothing refills while it is held; silence gives a
    home back each beat; the right face, with nothing threatening, spends nothing standing.
  - `test the sprint: the right face held while walking runs at a quarter of the speed of sound, a
    home a bar` Sprint spends a home a bar and, run out, drops to a run with Hum winded: no note
    until the breath is full again.
  - `test a walk home that takes the whole breath, landed, grows it by one` Three steps home on a
    breath of three, landed on the downbeat: the breath holds four.
  - `owed` A test: the wheels spend an eighth of a home a bar and the mood wheel's jump a home
    (built in `game/src/world/world.zig`); the glide when it is built; guarding when it is built.
  - `owed` The ferry makes eight.
  - `owed` A frame: the breath shown on Hum as its lines, one for each home it holds, lit from the
    bottom as far as it has, panting while winded (built in `game/src/dark.zig`), never as a bar
    (`texture.md`).
- **Asks:** Did running out of breath feel like a body, or like a meter?
