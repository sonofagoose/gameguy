_Part of the specification (`../SPEC.md`, section 11): who lives round the lake. Ninety-six lives in
twelve lands, the voices that travel with you, the one who remembers you, and the travellers. A
land's score, written when its stage is built, fills each life in; it may add to this but not take
away._

## How a life works

- **Everyone is made of something ordinary:** a peg, a marble, a bottle cap, a screen door. Nobody
  has a face. Nobody is explained.
- **A tune.** Each life has a short tune of its own, sung in its own vowel, and a body that is the
  figure of its note against home.
- **A round.** Where it is on which morning. Most stay in one place. Some go round three, four, five
  or seven places, one a morning; a few take twelve, thirteen or seventeen. A round is a sum from
  the morning's number, so the almanac can show any morning to come.
- **A want.** An ending its tune can't reach by itself.
- **Helping** is the day's five steps:
  1. **Notice** who is there today.
  2. **Capture:** lean in while they sing, and keep the phrase.
  3. **Extract:** on the page, a kept phrase shows what it is made of and where it stops short.
  4. **Select:** find what it is missing. It is always somewhere: another life, another morning, a
     place, a wonder, one of your own echoes.
  5. **Sing:** at dusk, when the land gathers, sing with them and land the ending.
- **What it changes.** Their part joins the land's dusk song for good. They lend you a small voice:
  one colour of one of the five. The river sings them in your night.
- **Each land's eight are its band:** a ground, a pulse, a tune, a third, and four colours. A land
  with all eight sings its whole song at dusk. Twelve songs, ninety-six parts.
- **A life goes on past its first ending** (`years.md`): two more wants, ties to other lives, duets
  when they meet, a morning you can live as it, and over years a change into something that keeps
  its tune.
- **Nothing here is a chore list.** Nobody asks. A life sings what it sings, where it is, and the
  player who never helps anyone still reaches the thirteenth morning. The lands are thinner for it,
  and the lake is lower.

## The one who remembers

**The vine.** A flowering vine that remembers across the days. It is a figure of light, flat, until
you are in tune with it, and then it has a body.

- It hums back what you let go, a little wrong, the way someone remembers a song.
- Its voice lives on the third, the one sweetness no stack of your own fifths can make.
- Its round is by thirds: it shares your sun every fourth morning, at the Garden (1), the Stage (5),
  the Hollow (9) and the Garden (13).
- Who it is, and what it is called, is Morgan's. Left blank, each player hears someone of their own.

## The ones that aren't people

- **Wolves** are made of the comma: doubled outlines, the only red in the world in their eyes, a
  howl that beats. They come up from under the wheel at dusk. Brought home, each folds into a pup
  that follows you and drums.
- **Knots** are fear: two rhythms tugging against each other. Stand where they meet.
- **Greys** are what the Grid makes of a creature: flat, on the grid exactly, drawn in stairs. They
  walk only on ground that has set grey. Swing breaks their step, and standing a third away brings
  their colour back.
- **The Wolf,** in the crack at the Seam, is every echo you turned away from. It is tamed by letting
  each one go.
- **The Grid** is not a creature. It is a day that changed nothing.

## On the thirteenth morning

Every round lines up and everyone is at the garden. Whatever parts you gathered are there, and the
twelve songs are one song. With all ninety-six it is the fullest sound the game makes. With none it
is Hum, the vine and the voices, and that is enough to end on.
