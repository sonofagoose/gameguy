## 4. "freezing and heated…" (poem)

~~~text
freezing and heated 
winter sun come on 
when autumn leaves
your feet stirred up 
in twirling shapes 
a figure of speech
on wind over weeds 
between our porch and the streets 

offer peace and release 
at last 
showing up
to show off the contrast

mail to my heart
an apple that is peeled
the petals of verbena
a purple dress and heels
carefully curated
collections of reels
she sends me intently
the melting of steel

a garden in my field
gold and radiant one
turn the fiery wheel
careful, together

ride with me a while
live a loving dream of life 
see I'm off in style when I die
life is FLASHING before our eyes
~~~
