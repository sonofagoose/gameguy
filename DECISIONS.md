# Decisions

_Every question the documents left open, decided, with the reason. Part 0 of the brainstorm asks for
this file: "decide them yourself in line with the why below, and record each decision and its
reason". Decided on 2026-11-19 with `SPEC.md`, and added to as the game is built, newest at the end.
Morgan can overturn any of them; the ones that are his by right are marked **his**, with the default
taken until he says otherwise._

## New decisions

|---|---|---|

| Tables or laws? | Laws. No effect is looked up that the seven laws could produce | what connects every idea; lets things nobody listed work |
| Does Hum ever choose notes? | After the thirteenth morning, seated | keeps what the diaries loved, and answers the choir director's "it never once let me bend a note" |

| How many lives? | Eight to a land: ninety-six | a band's worth for each land's song; few enough to know |

| Language | Zig | the cross-build, the headless run and the same-take proof exist in it |
| Reuse the toys' code? | What they proved, not what they are (`spec/engine.md`) | the toys' synth, rules and drawing don't scale to the world |
| What is the clock? | The sound card's sample count | one clock for the world and the sound means they can't drift |
| How fast does the world step? | Sixty times a second, with hands stamped to the millisecond and Hum's breath starting within a buffer | the instrument answers at once; the world stays cheap. If hands say it feels late, 120 |
| One sound engine or several? | One bank of resonators for everything | it is what the world is made of; measured at 2,100 overtones a core |
| The backlight | The game sets it: dark in the dark, breathing a little with the music | measured: it is over half the idle draw and can be written every frame. Eyes decide the breathing |
