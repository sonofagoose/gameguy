# Decisions

_Every question the documents left open, decided, with the reason. Part 0 of the brainstorm asks for
this file: "decide them yourself in line with the why below, and record each decision and its
reason". Decided on 2026-11-19 with `SPEC.md`, and added to as the game is built, newest at the end.
Morgan can overturn any of them; the ones that are his by right are marked **his**, with the default
taken until he says otherwise._

## New decisions

| question | decided | why |
|---|---|---|
| The name | "Oh. You're in." as a working title. **His** | the first line said in it; the line he liked; the diaries' players made "in" their word. Others: "Thirteenth Morning"; "Safe and Sound", which is his own title; "Hum" |
| Tables or laws? | Laws. No effect is looked up that the seven laws could produce | what connects every idea; lets things nobody listed work |
| Does Hum ever choose notes? | After the thirteenth morning, seated | keeps what the diaries loved, and answers the choir director's "it never once let me bend a note" |
| When does the lean arrive? | The fifth morning, at the Stage | the blues is where a note leans |
| How many lives? | Eight to a land: ninety-six | a band's worth for each land's song; few enough to know |
| How many wonders? | Twelve to a land: 144 | one for each law and five more in every land |
| Language | Zig | the cross-build, the headless run and the same-take proof exist in it |
| Reuse the toys' code? | What they proved, not what they are (`spec/engine.md`) | the toys' synth, rules and drawing don't scale to the world |
| What is the clock? | The sound card's sample count | one clock for the world and the sound means they can't drift |
| How fast does the world step? | Sixty times a second, with hands stamped to the millisecond and Hum's breath starting within a buffer | the instrument answers at once; the world stays cheap. If hands say it feels late, 120 |
| One sound engine or several? | One bank of resonators for everything | it is what the world is made of; measured at 2,100 overtones a core |
| The backlight | The game sets it: dark in the dark, breathing a little with the music | measured: it is over half the idle draw and can be written every frame. Eyes decide the breathing |
| The lights on the case | Not used | measured: they ignore writes on this system |
| Rumble | Out | Morgan, 2026-11-19: "there are no vibration/rumble mechanisms". The line can be driven and nothing is on it |
| A voice in | Out. The game never listens to the room | Morgan, 2026-11-19: "the microphone input thing is gimmicky and is never a mechanism to lean on when the underlying game and experience and world are properly fleshed out" |
| An instrument over USB | Later; nothing depends on it | the kernel has the drivers; only the black can host so far |
| Real time | Never used | measured: the handheld doesn't know how long it was off |
| Where the game lives | On its own card when there is one; where it is installed when there isn't | `design/the-card.md` |
| Agents | One owner. An agent only for bounded side-work and for grading a stage, never more than two | Part 0; and two max-effort agents have died on the usage limit |
| Where the build runs | A fresh session opened with the brief in `SPEC.md` section 22, unless Morgan says to go on in this one | Part 0: the builder starts from the specification, not from a session full of drafts |
| How is a claim kept from being dropped, shrunk or built and not felt? | A tool reads every sentence of the specification as a claim; a promise speaks for the sentences it quotes, with its full size and its cheat; the build works out each rung from evidence and refuses a stage called done before its claims are kept (`spec/promises.md`) | Morgan, 2026-11-19: "a promise tracking guard that dynamically works for every claim/description/design". A hand-typed list only guards what someone remembered to type |
| How big is the world? | One to a hundred. Each land about 3 square kilometres, the lake 3.7 km across, the whole 7.7 km across (`spec/size.md`) | the atlas said each land was "as big as a country to Hum" and then sized one at a minute and a half's run. Asters as tall as cathedrals are literally true at one to a hundred: a real aster is 0.9 to 1.85 m and Ulm's spire is 161.5 m |
| What is the card for? | Two things a light game can afford: nothing is a copy, and nothing is forgotten. Every thing its own shape; the ground keeps the light of what was sung on it; every dawn's save kept; each day's song as sound; friends' worlds kept (`spec/size.md`) | Morgan, 2026-11-19: "why shouldn't it take up all of 32GB/64GB?" and "I don't want anything to be held back just for the sake of optimization". Lines and resonators are light by nature, so the room goes to uniqueness and memory, which a player feels, and not to padding |
| Is there a picture on the card at all? | One: the ground's light, painted by play | the look is lines of light, so there is nothing to paint except what the world remembers. Measured: the GPU samples 530 million dots a second and the screen needs 18 million |
| How deep are the people? | Three wants each, ties across the lake, a duet for every pair that meets, a morning you can live as any life you have helped, and over years lives that change and hand their tunes down (`spec/years.md`) | Morgan, 2026-11-19: seven years with one game before finishing it, and this "should vastly vastly absolutely immensely surpass that". Depth by rule and by time, because music can grow, meet and be handed down, and written scenes can't |
| Do lives change over years? | Yes; Morgan's call | "Nothing dies. Things transform." And every dawn is kept, so the first ones can always be walked beside again |
| What carries two-console play? | The cable: one world, two players. A visit, and a whole world for two begun apart in the dark (`spec/together.md`) | Morgan, 2026-11-19: "we can't count on two unconnected console's speakers or two untrained people's ears to carry the weight of the multiplayer experience" |
| Are R1 and R2 told apart? | Yes, in what they do and how they look (`SPEC.md` 9): R1's wheel jumps home to any wedge while time thickens, drawn large on the ground from above; R2's swell steps home to a neighbour on the downbeat, shown only on Hum. **Proposed, his call:** the swell's steps move to L1 and L2, tapped while R2 is held, so the two wheels share no stick, and the right stick keeps the lean and the look. L1 held stays the voice wheel when R2 is not held | Morgan, 2026-11-20, after playing the wheel toy: "I really like what is going on with the whole R1/R2 wheel stuff but the two need more visual and actionable differentiation... maybe even consider L1/L2 as well?" In the toy both move home with the right stick, so they blur. Thumbs: R2 the right index, L1 and L2 the left, the left thumb walks, the right thumb looks |
| The comma in real air | Out | it rests on two unlinked speakers and a trained ear |
| How is height made to be felt on a small screen? | A low horizon and a small Hum while walking; at a tall thing's foot the view leans back and opens wide, so Hum and the top are in one frame; joints up the stalk to count; and time: a call takes almost three seconds to climb | the first frames read as a meadow of ordinary flowers (`spec/promises/seen.md`) |
