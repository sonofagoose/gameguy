# Decisions

_Every question the documents left open, decided, with the reason. Part 0 of the brainstorm asks for
this file: "decide them yourself in line with the why below, and record each decision and its
reason". Decided on 2026-11-19 with `SPEC.md`, and added to as the game is built, newest at the end.
Morgan can overturn any of them; the ones that are his by right are marked **his**, with the default
taken until he says otherwise._

| How is roughness measured? | Sethares's sum over every pair of partials in a frame, the two amplitudes times his curve, the loudest partial as one; a frame is rough past halfway between a whole tone and a half step of equal pure tones at 440 Hz | Vassilakis's weighting counts a pair 20 dB down almost as much as the loudest, so a dense consonant texture read as rough; with the product a quiet pair counts as little as it sounds |

With headphones in, by the playback path, none of it | `SPEC.md`: "The speaker is the floor. Low voices are built from upper overtones and the ear supplies the root." The usual shortcut drives the low band through a distortion, whose intermodulation is out of tune in a world that is pure; moving energy into a note's own harmonics keeps every partial a harmonic of something sounding. The ears measured 6% of the evening in the speaker's band before and 69% after; the cost on the purple is 0.9 microseconds a buffer |
