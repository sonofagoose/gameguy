## What the audit's tensions point at

I read the audit as a map of things that cannot all be true at once. Its "winner" lines measure
against an order of files that Morgan never held. Two notes on its facts:

- **Its theory item 6 comes to the wrong conclusion.** It says eleven fifths is not the wolf. Eleven
  pure fifths fold to 521.505 cents, and the Pythagorean wolf fifth is 678.495 cents. They add up to
  1200: one is the other turned upside down. So an echo eleven fifths behind you does make the wolf
  with you, as the old Volta table said. (C up to E sharp is also an augmented third, not an
  augmented fourth.) The sweet-thirds wolf, 737.637 cents, is a different animal, as the audit says.
- **Its eleven "missed mechanics" are mostly one thing.** The D that cannot please both its
  neighbours, the wolf you could place, and the three ways a major chord turns minor are all views
  of the grid that fifths and thirds make together. Shape 1 named that grid on the first day, and it
  was then put off until after the ending.

### 1. The two wheels are one wheel

The audit's sharpest contradiction is that the wheel is two things: seven moods inside a key
(`the-wheel.md`), and twelve keys with their relative minors (shape 1, and the wheel as now built).
The build shows twelve major letters over a model that has seven homes, so the letter D plays D
minor.

They are the same wheel. Put the seven homes of C's notes on the ordinary two-ring circle of fifths:

| home | its mode | its chord | where it sits | the mood's name so far |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| F | Lydian | major | outer ring, one to the left | wonder |
| C | Ionian | major | outer ring, here | sunny home |
| G | Mixolydian | major | outer ring, one to the right | drive |
| D | Dorian | minor | inner ring, under F | cool soul |
| A | Aeolian | minor | inner ring, under C | tenderness |
| E | Phrygian | minor | inner ring, under G | fierceness |
| B | Locrian | diminished | the thin edge past E minor | the darkest |

Three bright cells on the outside, the three under them, and an edge. That is how noodles lights a
key, as the first brainstorm describes it: IV, I and V on the outside, ii, vi and iii beneath them,
and the diminished chord as a thin wedge "living on the sector's edge". And it is wasatch's rule:
the notes in play are seven places on the circle, and home is which of them you stand on.

- **Proposal: light the wheel as noodles lights it.** Under the thumb that is three directions and
  two depths, which is the half push Morgan already asked for (`SCRATCH.md`). The moods he confirmed
  by ear stay as they are; only their places change. The letters become true.
- **The same picture is the map.** If the lands are the outer ring and what is underneath is the
  inner ring, then where you stand is which cells are lit. "The actual entire world is a gigantic
  circle of fifths" (Morgan, `SCRATCH.md`) becomes literal, and the stick at rest is the middle of
  the lake. A picture of it was drawn for Morgan with this note and is not in the repository; in
  letters, standing in the Garden:

```
      by day, on the shore:      F     C     G
      at night, underneath:      Dm    Am    Em    B°
                                    the island
```

- **Walking is changing key.** Cross into the next land and the lit patch slides one cell. One note
  changes (F becomes F sharp), and what was "drive" from the Garden is "sunny home" once you are
  there. A mood is then a lean toward a neighbouring place and hour: wonder is the Porch's home
  heard through the Garden's notes.
- **The patch is an instrument on the first evening.** With two cells awake you already have home
  and the amen. With three you have the three chords that carry most songs. This is where
  "performance from the first evening" could live: you never pick a bare pitch, and you always pick
  a home. Nothing can sound wrong, and everything is chosen.

It would have to be true in the hands that six cells and an edge can be played without looking. That
is experiment 2.

### 2. Underneath

Morgan's newest thread puts each major land's relative minor underneath it. The audit is right that
the old deep (the mirror) is a different thing, and it worries about 24 worlds. Three things already
in the notebook say what underneath could be.

- **A relative minor is the same notes with another home.** Morgan's style book says "the car at 2am
  and the garden at noon are the same figure, seen at different hours". The atlas already gives
  every land a noon face and a 2am face.
- **`the-wheel.md` already made that home the one that remembers:** tenderness, home on the sixth,
  the power called Remember.
- **The river already sings the day back.**

**Proposal: night is the relative minor of the day.** Underneath is the same land heard from its
other home. The river carries you down through it, and its song is your day with home moved a pure
minor third lower (5/3 against 2, 315.641 cents down). That makes travel, sleep and dream one trip,
which is what Morgan described, and it needs no second set of lands. Whether it works is only a
question for ears: does the same tune over the lower home sound like the day remembered? That is
sketch b.

Two true things come with it, for later:

- **Underneath is a real direction, and it disagrees with round.** The A a pure minor third under
  the Garden's C is 5/3. The A three lands round the shore is 27/16. They are 21.506 cents apart
  (81/80, the other comma). So the inner ring is not the outer ring's notes again: it is its own
  ring, lower by a hair.
- **There are three ways down, and each moves one voice.** In place: C major to A minor, G rises to
  A. To the right: C major to E minor, the Alfalfa's night, C falls to B. Through the mirror: C
  major to C minor, E falls to E flat, and that is the night of the land three round to the left.
  The mirror does turn C's chord over into C minor, so the Mirror Pool could be that third door. The
  audit's "four different structures" would be three doors and one drawer (the country with no
  octave).

### 3. The comma is not a date, and not a monster

The notebook has the comma arrive on the thirteenth morning, and it gives the two ways of living
with it two villains and three endings. The audit says this flattens temperament into moral
opposites, and I agree. Three proposals take its place.

**a. Heard as silence. Home stops answering.**

Law 1 says how much two notes share: all of it when they match, 98% at 2 cents, under a tenth at a
comma (`spec/laws.md`; the width of 15 cents is a number I chose, to tune). So walk the whole shore
by pure fifths, true at every step, carrying your own tuning and taking no step down at the Seam,
and you come back to the Garden 23.460 cents warm. Call, as you did on the first evening. The garden
answers at 8.7%. It stays almost dark.

That is the first verb of the game turned over, and it is Morgan's own line from The Generative
Self: "I keep missing home / by something I can't measure". No sound in it is out of tune. The comma
is heard as an absence, which may be the answer to the held comma that is "really close" and not yet
landing.

Come down half the comma and the garden answers at 54%. If someone else comes up half, the two of
you are in tune with each other, and each of you is half lit to your own. The poem's "meet in the
middle" then has numbers. That is sketch a.

This needs no calendar. It happens whenever a player closes a lap, in either direction, and a lap
one way undoes a lap the other.

**b. Felt as work. The remainder is an engine.**

Take the oldest turnaround there is: C, A minor, D minor, G, and home. Tune every chord pure, and
let each keep a note of the one before. Home comes back at 80/81, which is 21.506 cents low. Every
chord was in tune, and you sank. Four laps is 86.025 cents, nearly a half step. Play it the other
way round (C, G, D minor, A minor, C) and you rise by the same amount. On the treaty's notes the
same four chords come back exactly, and nothing moves.

So a circuit of pure chords is a ratchet. In a world where height is pitch, it is a pump. The poem
says "ache IS the engine", and The Generative Self says "A closed system has nowhere to go". Here
both are literal: the pure circuit does work because it does not close, and the tempered one closes
and does none.

- **Different circuits are different gears** (heights at 100 m to the octave, the notebook's number,
  to tune):

| circuit | steps | each lap moves home | as height |
|---|---|---|---|
| eight fifths down, then a pure third down | 9 | 1.954 cents down | 0.16 m |
| C, A minor, D minor, G, each keeping a note of the last | 4 | 21.506 cents down | 1.79 m |
| the whole shore by fifths | 12 | 23.460 cents up | 1.96 m |
| three pure major thirds up | 3 | 41.059 cents down | 3.42 m |
| four pure minor thirds up | 4 | 62.565 cents up | 5.21 m |

- **On the wheel, the turnaround is a small loop of the thumb:** here, under, under and left, over
  and right, here. It is a crank. Turn it one way and the thing you are holding sinks; the other way
  and it rises.
- **It keeps the ear rule.** Each chord rings pure. Only where you end up has moved.
- **The human moment** is doing the same loving thing every day, in tune at every step, and finding
  yourself somewhere else.

Sketch c is the turnaround, four laps pure and two on the treaty's notes. Experiment 4 is the crank
in the hand.

**c. The treaty is ground, not a villain.**

Pure ground rings fully, and it carries you off. Treaty ground answers everything a little less (98%
on a fifth, 43% on a third) and keeps you level, so everyone can meet on it. Both are tools. Neither
is a sin. Which one you stand on can be chosen step by step: at a border, step pure and keep the
ring, or give two cents and stay home.

### 4. The island, and the ice

The atlas has no island; the audit says so. But the poem puts the treaty in the middle: equal
temperament is "a 'center' of the space", and "One surface, upon which all agree and the blooming
people can meet in the middle".

- **Proposal: the island is the middle of the wheel.** It is where the stick rests, the same
  distance from every land, and the one place where all twelve lands' music can sound together, a
  little dulled. That would make it the place where what you keep from each land can be played as
  one piece, which is what the last freedom, composing, needs. The Seam can stay on the far shore as
  the place where the shore's two ends miss.
- **The ice is Morgan's image, and I do not know what it is to him.** Two readings, both proposals.
  As a thing to do: ice is a plate, and a plate shows still lines when it is sung to, so the frozen
  lake is the puddle bridge at the size of the world, and the way across depends on what you sing.
  (At the puddle he trusted the lines and then asked "what's this even for?" This would be what it
  is for.) As what it means: the frozen part of the lake repeats exactly, the dry part forgets, and
  the flowing part remembers and changes. A lake that is low and partly frozen then holds both of
  the notebook's dangers at once: nothing comes home, and nothing changes.

### 5. Depth as one law, not a list

The audit asks whether 96 lives and 288 endings are a checklist under another name. Partly they are.
Many of the lives are good because the help they want is something music does: a steady pulse, the
note two rhythms share, a third from someone else. Others are errands (the sock, the clapper).

**Proposal: the depth is his sister's sentence, as a law. The world keeps what you play.** Anything
that can ring keeps the rhythm of what reached it for a while, and gives it back in its own voice,
in tune with home. Move home and everything you have laid down changes chord with you. How long
things remember is the lake's level, which the notebook already says ("a low lake forgets"). The
long shape of the game is then silence becoming song: nothing remembers at first, and at the end the
whole world is singing what you gave it, and you go round harmonizing. A knot is a memory that got
tangled.

It would also make the wheel's effect heard everywhere, which is what his eight minutes with the
tangles were missing. Whether a garden full of your own bars is a band or a mess is experiment 3.

### 6. Put together

One paragraph, all of it a proposal:

You wake in the dark with one note. You call, something answers, you fall into step with it, and the
page stands up. From then on the world is an instrument that keeps what you play. It is laid out as
the wheel in your hand, so where you stand is which chords you have, night is the same land heard
from underneath, and walking is changing key one note at a time. Pure ground rings and carries you
off. The middle of the lake is where everyone can meet, a little dulled. Walk all the way round,
true at every step, and your own home stops answering you, by something you can't measure. What you
do about that, alone or with someone, is the ending. After it you can put home anywhere.

That last line is the poem's two choices as the last freedom: WHERE home is, and HOW HIGH. If the
lands are tuned as a chain of fifths from wherever home is, then moving home moves the seam.
