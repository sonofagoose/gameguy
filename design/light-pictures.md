# Light-pictures: the garden's story, without words

A draft for Morgan's eye before any of it is drawn (plan of 2026-11-21, step 5). SPEC.md 17 keeps
the game's words to "Oh. You're in." and Jeanne's three lines, so the garden tells what happened to
it in pictures. Each picture rises in faint lines of light when Hum stands still at its place for a
bar, in the page's style (a few strokes, drawn as the oscilloscope's beam draws), plays once in
about eight seconds, and sinks back. Standing again replays it. Nothing on the screen but the
picture.

1. **At the great aster's foot: the garden before.** The bowl from above, as the page shows it, but
   whole and green: the hose running straight from the spigot to the pool, water in it, the asters
   younger, small figures of light (lives, not yet met) along the shore. Then the picture darkens
   from its edges.
2. **At the bridge puddle's edge: the storm.** Rain as falling ticks, faster than Drip's. The
   puddles opening as rings on the bowl, each ring a note. The hose, struck, jerking.
3. **At the hose's knot: the knot.** The hose tangling on itself in three loops that become the
   three knots, out of step with each other; the water stopping behind them; the pool going still
   and black.

Questions for Morgan: are these the story you want the garden to have? Who are the small figures
along the shore (they could be his to fill, like the figure on the pool's edge)?
