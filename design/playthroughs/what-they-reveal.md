# What the seven playthroughs reveal

These players are imagined and all seven finish. Trust the diaries on what the canon makes vivid and
where it contradicts itself, not on how it sounds or sits in the hand.

## 1. What worked for nearly everyone

- **Getting "in" with Drip** (all seven). The first verb is timing a breath, which a nurse, a
  drummer and a nine-year-old already own, and the reward is a note neither of you sang. Later
  another skill from life, laying back, breaks the Grid (five diaries).
- **The swell, held and let go on the one.** The one musical choice left to the player is
  conducting. Imani: "It took away my notes and left me my job."
- **The river:** the day sung back, four kept, the rest walking into the water.
- **The vine humming yesterday back a little wrong.** Six diaries heard someone of their own (Dev,
  Mom, Bev, Grandma, a choir, a violin teacher). Left blank, the beloved is a mirror.
- **Holding the comma.** Six diaries took a bleak offer first, and the river's wordless second offer
  brought each back. Keep them takeable: they taught by being lived in.
- **One model for ear and eye.** Each sense covered for the other: Walt navigated by key changes,
  Theo played muted, Kai drew the circle of fifths.

The core is narrower than the canon: you never choose a note, only a moment, and the world answers
with a sound that belongs to nobody.

## 2. Where it broke

**Real flaws, worst first**

1. **The eleventh dusk is an ambush** (Rosa, Theo, Walt, Priya, Kai). The cause is eleven days back,
   the cue a fade Kai read as illness, the only hint a line Walt took to mean the boat. Kai and Walt
   left for five and nine days; Walt, a widower, heard a sermon. Priya caught it firing eight fifths
   out: "First number in this game that's decoration."
2. **The Grid punishes what the game asks for** (Rosa, Theo, Ash, Priya). Repeat, Fierce's takes to
   beat and gates that need setup days all grey the ground. "It reads my footsteps, not my reasons."
3. **Whole systems go untouched.** No diary opens the wheel in a fight, and the canon's combat
   section never mentions it, though Morgan wants it as the main tool. Nobody uses a borrowed chord,
   a unison guard, Break or a pool's memory. Of the five flat-side lands only the Porch is named:
   mornings eight to twelve rise over one voice and no boss.
4. **Gentle widens the beat, not the ledges.** Bea needed Ash for the awning on day one and the
   ridges in week two. The thumb audit fails twice: gliding wants a face button and the camera
   stick, and Theo held lock-on with his middle finger "so my index can clap".
5. **Eyes and first inputs.** Out of tune is drawn as blur, so Walt thought his eyes had gone; R3,
   the first required input, is the hardest to find (Kai) and press (Walt).
6. **Words carry what the world should.** Kai skipped Box Elder, Puffball, Jeanne and the three
   offers, and took his first ending unknowingly.
7. **The beloved is off the fighting road** (Theo), and **keeping four gives no feedback** (one
   drowsy pick cost Rosa a gate and nine days).

**Taste, not flaws:** Imani's wish to bend a note, her six days of triads and her "Pure isn't
alone"; Bea's wish to be heard; Theo's two minutes of black; Kai's fright at the wolves.

## 3. Tensions

Without trade-offs? In fights, after the first river, yes: Gentle or Fierce, chosen nightly.
Elsewhere, not yet.

| tension | where it gives | smallest change |
|---|---|---|
| calm / challenge | Space (Bea); a first fight where four skilled players heard no ceiling ("So it's playing itself?") | Gentle widens landings too; a strike you time yourself rings fuller than a placed one |
| depth / approachability | The eleven-day rule: depth with no surface | Age echoes by fifths travelled, visibly from the eighth |
| keeping / letting go | The lesson reaches only those who come back | Released echoes stay in the pools, sung up in blue: giving, as the Caravan's trade was for Kai |
| practice / sameness | Four players told off for method | Grey grows only where a Repeat retraces an earlier run in step, shows underfoot, and lifts when the day ends differently |
| alone / together | Split parts thinned nobody (four pairs), but consoles with no treaty clash, fights flam, and a clap on two fails silently | The clap is the one wherever it lands; treaty fights subdivide the beat; carve the treaty on the river post; over the cable, one clock |

## 4. Did the diaries keep the rules?

Closely.

- **Sourness as normal.** Ash: "First evenings were two radios, her key and mine." Two keys in one
  room for evenings: the one sustained clash in any diary, and the default for two consoles. Theo:
  "no tune ever finishes. Like a sneeze that won't come." In tune, but weeks of withheld endings
  test the rule's spirit.
- **The comma itself.** Every diary hears a slow swell. A comma beats about 3.6 times a second at
  middle C and slows only as pitch drops, where this speaker's low voices are upper harmonics that
  beat faster. Nobody with ears has checked the ending.
- **Undrawable:** nothing. The trouble runs the other way. Walt: "a thread of light I'll take on
  faith".
- **Faces:** none, but by not looking: Jeanne is a "girl" in two diaries, described in none.
- **Text:** the letter holds ("There are, a line at a time"); the spirit doesn't: "It's all words. I
  press X till they stop."
- **Zelda, Bee and Puppycat:** no names, places or characters. Nearest brush: the canon's white cat
  that sings, which is Morgan's own.

## 5. Decisions forced, ranked

1. **The eleventh dusk:** ambush, or a loss you watch coming? The Wolf gave four of the five their
   take back to release, so keep the loss and warn in the Wolf's own language (a doubling outline, a
   red glint, a second of beating).
2. **What the Grid measures:** repeats, footsteps, or a day that changes nothing. Only the last fits
   Repeat, Fierce and the gates.
3. **The wheel in a fight, and the flat side.** Give moods work against enemies and mornings eight
   to twelve something to face, or admit the game the diaries describe: rhythm, voices, a river.
4. **The beloved** (Morgan's): keep the blank; put a corner of their triangle where the fighting is.
   Six diaries saw the blue figure on the pool's edge; none learned who.
5. **Eyes, ears and hands:** large type (one line need not be small), dissonance never as blur
   alone, nothing essential in stereo or bass (pairs play in mono), focus by standing still, lock-on
   that latches.
6. **The duet's third voice.** Theo heard it "under both of us, low"; Priya "between", half a comma
   up, which is what two near-unisons sum to. Adopt hers if Morgan's ears agree.

## 6. Three prototypes

1. **The dark, Drip, the wheel, one knot.** Settles the core: whether locking to a tick feels like
   being answered through this speaker; whether inks snapping into register read at 229 ppi or are
   taken for blur; whether a snapped strike feels like power and the wheel earns a place mid-fight;
   and, to Morgan's ears, whether the third note and the comma's seconds are beautiful.
2. **One day, one night, one echo.** Record a minute, keep it, change the world, walk beside
   yourself. Settles whether "a take is its inputs" survives a changed world (Rosa's gate, Bea's
   rescue and every traded card rest on it), whether a chord of three selves feels like a band, and
   whether grey underfoot and an ageing echo read in daylight.
3. **The treaty, with and without the cable.** The black unit is back and the wire is proven (half a
   millisecond round trip, nothing lost; `docs/hardware-target.md`), but so far only the black can
   host, so the clap must stand alone. Unproven: how closely two units land a clap, how long before
   drift becomes a flam, whether split parts sound whole alone and warm together, and whether the
   third tone exists at a usable volume.
